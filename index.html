Head-to-head showdowns. Two teams, one problem, first correct answer wins the round.

Elimination Gauntlet

06

Estimation Auction

No exact solving, just your best guess. Closest team scores, exact answers earn a bonus.

07

Hands-Up Steal

One question, whole room. First hand up gets it, miss it and it's open for anyone to steal.

08

Speed Sprint

One team, one 3-minute clock. Blast through as many problems as you can before time runs out.

09

Pyramid Race

Same pyramid, one timer, teams race to the top, partial credit is awarded if time runs out.

10

Streak Vault

One team in the spotlight, points double each round, bank it or push your luck for more.

11

Swap Market

Teams bid swap tokens for the one shared easier backup problem, highest bidder gets it.

12

All-In Wager

Teams secretly wager 1–5 points on their confidence, right answers pay out, wrong ones cost you.

13

Curse Card

Answer first, then secretly saddle another team with a curse for their next round. Survive it and score a bonus.

14

Scapegoat

Every team secretly names another team as their scapegoat each round. If your scapegoat misses the problem, you steal half their points.

15

Point Heist

A shared vault sits center stage. Correct answers let you draw from it or raid whoever's winning outright.

16

Hot Potato

A single potato grows in value as it's passed around. Answer right to pass it on, just don't be holding it when the cutoff hits.

17

Bounty

The current leader gets marked. Answer first to bank your own points, or collect a cut straight from them instead.

18

Sequence Detective

A sequence with the ending hidden. Teams have to spot the pattern and guess what comes next.

19

Siege

Every correct answer forks between Fortress and Army. Fortify to score, Reinforce to shield up, or Attack to raid another team's Fortress.

20

The Pool

Every team seals a prediction for the final standings up front. Periodic checkpoints score matches against the live leaderboard.
